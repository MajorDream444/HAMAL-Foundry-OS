# Canonical Truth Governance

**Status:** ACTIVE  
**Owner:** AMA Solutions Corp

## Purpose

This repository separates vision, demonstrated capability, commercial authority, market evidence, and proposals.

A claim becomes canonical only when it:

1. has an explicit status label;
2. names its source and owner;
3. does not conflict with a higher-order record;
4. is approved in the appropriate register when material.

## Truth hierarchy

1. Signed agreements and authoritative regulatory records
2. Demonstrable code, tests, and production evidence
3. Approved Decision Register entries
4. Approved Capability Register entries
5. Primary external research
6. Internal strategy and proposals
7. Generated summaries, podcasts, decks, and marketing

Repetition does not create verification.

## Claim rules

- Planned functionality is written in the future or conditional tense.
- “Sovereign,” “compliant,” “immutable,” “automated,” and “buyer accepted” require scoped evidence.
- Policy alignment does not prove demand.
- Partner history is not automatically applicant history.
- A prototype does not prove production readiness.
- Human authorization remains required for consequential decisions.
- Derived AI output cannot verify its own source material.

## Change protocol

1. Create a branch.
2. Identify the affected truth domain.
3. Update the relevant register.
4. Link evidence or mark the claim PROPOSAL/OPEN.
5. Identify affected downstream materials.
6. Obtain review before merging.

## Conflict protocol

Label the matter **CONFLICT**, preserve both sources, suspend external use of the disputed claim, assign an owner and resolution test, and record the result.

## External outputs

Applications, proposals, sites, demos, decks, podcasts, and NotebookLM source packs should derive from the current `main` branch.
