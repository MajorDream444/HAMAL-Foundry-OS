## **4\. MobStack: The Orchestration and Governance Layer**

If Hanzo is the brain, **MobStack** is the nervous system. It is the orchestration layer that separates "Software" from "Consulting." While a consultant provides manual coaching and static reports (the "Chauffeur" model), MobStack is a "Self-Driving" automated workflow engine. The MobStack workflow follows a rigorous four-step sequence:

* **Assignment:** Tasks are routed based on diagnosed capability gaps and role requirements.  
* **Governed Execution:** Users execute tasks using AI agents operating under strict policy limits.  
* **Human Review:** A qualified supervisor performs a "human-in-the-loop" check to authorize or remediate work.  
* **Verified Receipt:** The system generates a cryptographic receipt of the decision, ensuring clear accountability.

This automated governance creates the "provenance" data that is recorded unchangeably in the next layer.

## **6\. Framework Success Metrics and "Pass Gate" Criteria**

Validation of the pilot is based on measurable efficiency gains for the procurement office, not just participant satisfaction.

### **Pilot KPIs**

* **Hours Saved in Document-Chasing:** Total reduction in manual verification time for the sponsor.  
* **Hours Saved in Sponsor Follow-up:** Reduction in back-and-forth communication regarding task status.  
* **First-Pass Acceptance Rate:** The percentage of SME work products accepted without remediation.  
* **Median Time to Evidence:** Duration from identifying a capability gap to generating buyer-accepted proof.

### **Implementation "Pass Gate" Discipline**

To preserve capital and maintain strategic rigor, the implementation must adhere to a strict PASS/HOLD/STOP protocol:

* **PASS:** Proceed to scale only if at least two buyers describe the same workflow failure and an accessible budget owner is identified.  
* **HOLD:** Pause if the sponsor is unwilling to share real operating requirements or if data residency remains unverified.  
* **STOP:** Terminate the project immediately if a budget owner cannot be found or if the pilot fails to demonstrate measurable hours saved in document-chasing.

The Capability-to-Contract model is the primary bridge to Qatar’s highly skilled future, replacing unverified assumptions with a foundation of auditable operational excellence.
