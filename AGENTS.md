# Instructions for Codex and AI Coding Agents

## Authority

This repository is the canonical truth layer for HAMAL and Foundry OS. Read files in this order:

1. `CANONICAL-TRUTH.md`
2. `governance/DECISION-REGISTER.md`
3. `governance/CAPABILITY-REGISTER.md`
4. `sources/SOURCE-POLICY.md`
5. The task-specific product, market, or research files

Higher-order records override narrative documents.

- Spell **HAMAL** exactly.
- HAMAL means **Hanzo + AMA (Art Mob AGInts) + Lux**.
- Never use HAMEL.
- AMA Solutions Corp is the commercialization and contracting lead unless signed documents say otherwise.
- Do not invent authority, partnerships, deployments, customers, certifications, licenses, revenue, or IP rights.

## Working rules

- Distinguish current capability from roadmap.
- Label material claims: VERIFIED, AVAILABLE, CONFIGURABLE, REPORTED, INFERENCE, PROPOSAL, OPEN, or CONFLICT.
- Do not promote a capability beyond the Capability Register.
- Record durable strategic changes in the Decision Register.
- Treat generated summaries, podcasts, and presentations as derived material, not corroborating evidence.
- Keep consequential actions human-authorized.
- Do not introduce unrelated verticals into Qatar work without an approved decision.
- Use branches and pull requests for canonical changes.
- Preserve source provenance and cite primary sources.
- If records conflict, stop and mark CONFLICT rather than choosing silently.

## Product posture

Evidence before code. For the Qatar wedge, do not represent market demand as validated until the Decision Register’s validation gate is met. Keep Foundry World optional and Mach II outside the first pilot unless a later decision changes this.
