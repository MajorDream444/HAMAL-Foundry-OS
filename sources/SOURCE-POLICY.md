# Source and Evidence Policy

## Source classes

| Class | Examples | Permitted use |
|---|---|---|
| A — Authoritative | Laws, regulator records, signed agreements, audited statements, official program terms | Verify legal, corporate, eligibility, and policy claims |
| B — Primary operational | Production code, tests, system logs, recorded customer interviews, written buyer acceptance | Verify capability and demand within stated scope |
| C — Credible secondary | Reputable research, industry reports, established journalism | Context and triangulation |
| D — Internal strategy | Memos, roadmaps, proposals, architecture concepts | Proposals and hypotheses |
| E — Derived/generated | NotebookLM outputs, podcasts, AI summaries, presentations | Ideation and critique only |
| F — Unverified | Informal introductions, screenshots without provenance, unattributed claims | Lead generation only |

## Citation requirements

Every material external claim should record:

- claim text;
- evidence label;
- source title and URL/path;
- source date;
- access/verification date;
- scope and limitations;
- owner responsible for re-verification.

## NotebookLM policy

Use NotebookLM to synthesize approved sources, identify contradictions, and generate questions. Do not add its own outputs back into the same evidence set as corroboration.

Recommended notebook order:

1. Canonical identity and governance
2. Capability and decision registers
3. Authoritative Qatar sources
4. Primary customer-validation evidence
5. Product specifications
6. Expansion cases

Exclude unrelated verticals from the core Qatar notebook unless the Decision Register promotes them.

## Market-validation minimum

Before a problem is described as validated, record:

- at least two independent buyers describing substantially the same costly workflow;
- the organizational owner of the problem;
- an identifiable budget or purchasing route;
- the evidence format the buyer would accept;
- one credible route to a supervised pilot.

Until then, label the thesis **PROPOSAL** or **OPEN**.
